SoundSafari: Pre-K Audio & Reading Academy

An interactive, sound-first educational web application designed to prepare pre-kindergarten and early elementary children for reading with phonics, word building, and speech pronunciation.

Learning Modules

  • 🔤 Phonics Jungle: Master A-Z letter sounds, digraphs, and phonemic awareness with guided audio feedback.
  • 🧁 CVC Word Bakery: Build 3-letter consonant-vowel-consonant words through interactive visual spelling tiles.
  • 🦁 Sentence Safari: Read and listen to 100+ decodable sentences with word-by-word audio narration.
  • 🎙️ Say & Play Studio: Interactive voice practice to encourage confident speech and pronunciation.
